Compliance technology

Tools that keep filings accurate

For IDs, GIS and financial statements

Intelligent document processing and OCR

When compiling requirements for SEC eSPARC, PCAB (audited financial statements and PRC licenses), or bank KYC and account opening, manual encoding leads to rejections over minor typos.

  • Mindox
    Built for the Philippine market. Extracts structured data from local documents, including barangay clearances, bank statements and scanned government IDs, in seconds.
  • ABBYY FineReader PDF
    Converts scanned notarized deeds, Articles of Incorporation and PCAB technical attachments into searchable, editable files. Also compares revised contract versions side by side.
For SEC, FDA and PCAB rules

Philippine legal and regulatory research AI

General AI chatbots often hallucinate outdated Philippine regulations. Use tools grounded in local jurisprudence and agency circulars.

  • JurisChat
    Searches over 120,000 Philippine legal authorities to answer compliance questions with citations. Drafts corporate documents such as Secretary's Certificates and Board Resolutions to Philippine standards.
  • Cassandra (Philippines Legal)
    Checks Revised Corporation Code rules, foreign equity limits under the Foreign Investments Act, and corporate governance obligations before filing with the SEC.
For checklists, FDA dossiers and cover letters

Enterprise AI assistants

For drafting cover letters, checking FDA License to Operate (LTO) and Certificate of Product Registration (CPR) ingredient lists, and cross-checking bank signatory matrices.

  • Microsoft 365 Copilot
    Or ChatGPT Enterprise. Upload agency checklists, such as PCAB Categorization and Classification tables or FDA Citizen's Charter requirements, into a private workspace. The assistant audits a client's folder for missing signatures, expired IDs or inconsistent business addresses before submission.
For government portals

Robotic process automation

Local portals such as SEC eSPARC, FDA ePortal and BIR or LGU systems lack public APIs.

  • UiPath
    Bots auto-fill repetitive client profile fields from your master Excel tracker while keeping audit logs.

Key compliance caveats

Data Privacy Act (RA 10173). Never upload client TINs, passport scans or bank records into free public AI tools. Use only enterprise plans with zero-data-retention guarantees, to stay compliant with the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

Human in the loop. Always have a compliance officer verify generated Secretary's Certificates and regulatory citations before notarization or portal submission.
